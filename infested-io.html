HOW TO PLAY
Move

WASD (ZQSD on AZERTY keyboards) or the arrow keys. On touch screens, touch anywhere and drag: a joystick appears where you touch.

Attack

Your weapons fire on their own. Just stay alive and keep moving.

Level up

Collect XP gems to level up and pick a new skill or support item (keys 1–3 work too).

Evolve

A ★5 skill plus its support item evolves when you open a chest from a mini-boss or boss.

Survive 15:00
